## 1. Vision Statement

> *"To create a world where personalized music therapy is accessible to everyone, powered by AI that truly understands human emotions."*

We envision a future where emotional well-being support is not limited by geography, income, or availability of trained professionals. Through the fusion of artificial intelligence and the timeless healing power of music, we will build technology that listens, understands, and responds to human emotions with therapeutic precision.

---

## 2. Mission Statement

> *"To democratize music therapy by building an open-source, AI-powered platform that detects emotions through multiple modalities and delivers scientifically grounded, personalized music experiences that promote mental health and emotional resilience."*

### How We Achieve This
1. **Detect** — Use AI to understand emotions through facial expressions, voice tone, and written text
2. **Recommend** — Match emotional states with therapeutic music using evidence-based algorithms
3. **Support** — Provide AI-guided therapy conversations for emotional guidance
4. **Track** — Enable users to monitor their emotional journey over time
5. **Open** — Build everything in the open, inviting global collaboration

|-----------|----------|----------|---------------|
| **Calm** | Brand recognition, meditation | No emotion detection | AI-powered personalization |
| **Headspace** | Content library, UX | Subscription-only, generic | Real-time emotion response |
| **Moodfit** | Mood tracking | Manual input only | Automatic emotion detection |
| **Woebot** | AI therapy chat | No music therapy | Multi-modal + music |
| **Spotify** | Music library | No therapy focus | Therapeutic music selection |
| **Endel** | Generative soundscapes | No emotion detection | Full therapy platform |

### Our Unique Position
We sit at the intersection of **emotion AI** and **music therapy** — a space no major player currently occupies. By being open-source, we also eliminate the barrier of subscription costs.
